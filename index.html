How the calculator works
New Activation = $5 + (PA % ÷ 10) + (Premium Loading % ÷ 10) + (NUA % ÷ 10), multiplied by new activations.
AIA = AIA units × (close rate % × $4), capped at $60 per AIA.
Accessories = accessory revenue × (accessory $/opp ÷ 1000). Example: $43.22/opp = 4.322%.
Connected = connected devices × (upgrades × $0.25).
HTP bonus = entered HTP %, capped at 15%. CSAT bonus = CSAT % ÷ 10 when CSAT is at least 90%; otherwise 0%.
Total compensation = production subtotal + HTP/CSAT bonus dollars.
